Kivi turns your voice dictations into a searchable personal memory. Today, dictation tools transcribe and forget — you're left scrolling through hundreds of notes trying to remember who said what about which project. Kivi solves this by using LLMs to extract structured knowledge from your dictation history: people, projects, preferences, and events. You ask Kivi a natural question like "summarise and send the dictation I did in a X meeting yesterday kivi does it all for you and sends it" and it answers from verified memory, not guesswork. Everything stays local and private. No cloud sync, no data sharing. Your words become knowledge you can actually use.
